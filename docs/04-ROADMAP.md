## Measurement (set up before Sprint 1 so you can prove lift)
- GA4 + Shopify analytics: CR, AOV, add-to-cart rate, homepage→PDP rate.
- Search Console: impressions/clicks on "[brand] Egypt" queries; rich-result coverage.
- PageSpeed/CrUX: LCP, CLS, INP (mobile) before vs. after Sprint 4.
- Heatmaps (e.g. Clarity — lightweight) to validate scroll depth & CTA clicks.

> Treat all percentage estimates in the audit as planning ranges; your own
> analytics are the source of truth. Ship, measure, iterate.
