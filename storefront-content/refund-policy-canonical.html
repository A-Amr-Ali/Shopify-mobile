Your consumer rights

Egyptian consumer-protection rules provide a 14-day return or exchange period for eligible goods without giving a reason, subject to statutory exceptions. These include goods whose nature, packaging or condition prevents return, and goods made to a customer's specifications. Eligibility is assessed for the specific item; a blanket exclusion does not apply to every product in our store.

Defective goods can be reported within 30 days of receipt. Where the law requires a return, replacement or refund, SOFIE will provide it without additional cost and within the applicable legal time limit. Refunds use the original payment method; the payment provider's posting time may differ.

Keep the product, packaging and proof of purchase while we review the request. Nothing in this policy limits statutory rights. Consumer Protection Agency guidance.
