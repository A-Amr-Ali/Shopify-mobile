Return & Refund Policy

At Sofie Store, customer satisfaction is important to us. Please review our policy below:

1. Return Policy

No Returns for Change of Mind:

We do not accept returns or exchanges for reasons other than product defects, damage, or expiration.

Eligible Returns:

Returns are accepted only if:

The product is defective or damaged upon delivery.

The product received is expired.

2. Reporting an Issue

If you receive a defective, damaged, or expired product, please contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order.

To process your request efficiently, please provide:

A clear description of the issue.

Photographic evidence showing the defect, damage, or expiration date.

Failure to report within 24 hours may affect eligibility for return or refund approval.

3. Refund Process

Once the issue is verified and approved, the refund will be processed to the original payment method within 15 to 30 business days.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider.

4. Exceptions

We are unable to accept returns that do not meet the criteria outlined above. We encourage customers to carefully review product details before completing their purchase.
