Store pickup

Eligible local-stock options can be collected free from SOFIE, The Place Mall, Gate 8, Mivida, New Cairo. Select pickup in your bag. Preparation is approximately 10 minutes during operating hours; wait for confirmation before travelling.

Standard delivery and by-order products

  • Standard in-stock products: 3–5 business days.
  • By-order products: 3–21 business days, unless a longer category or product-specific lead time applies.

Existing longer lead times for Home orders remain applicable: large appliances 25–40 days; large Home items in the existing “Value order” category 15–60 days; small items in that category 20–35 days. Please confirm the applicable lead time with our team before paying for these items. Business days exclude weekends and official holidays.

Mixed bags and delivery charges

If your bag contains SOFIE NOW options and standard-delivery items, they travel separately in the same order. The standard-delivery charge of EGP 150 is added to the SOFIE NOW fee. Pickup of eligible local items is free; any standard-delivery items remain subject to their delivery charge. Review the total shown in your bag and checkout before paying.

We will contact you if availability, shipping or customs delays change an estimate. For help confirming an option, address or lead time, WhatsApp SOFIE on +20 100 748 8707.
